Our promise

Tradition You Trust. Elegance You Love.

Established in 1981, Khurmi Jewellers serves customers from its showroom in Ellenabad, Haryana. We bring together gold, silver, diamond and bridal jewellery, along with helpful services for special occasions and everyday needs.

About Khurmi Jewellers

A local name with a lasting legacy

For us, jewellery is connected to family occasions, memories and milestones. Our aim is to help customers explore suitable designs and receive personal assistance at the showroom. All gold jewellery at Khurmi Jewellers is BIS hallmarked.
