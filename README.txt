KHURMI JEWELLERS WEBSITE

Open index.html directly in a modern browser. The separate Designs catalogue is linked from the homepage and opens as designs.html. Keep the complete folder structure together.

The catalogue contains 230 individual local design images extracted from the supplied 21-page DESIGNS.pdf: 59 Gold, 53 Silver, 58 Bridal and 60 Diamond. Search by name/code, filter by category, enlarge images, and use each design's WhatsApp enquiry link. Catalogue entries are enquiry references, not confirmation of live stock or prices.

The exact approved KJ logo supplied by the owner is used for all logo placements. No showroom interior or shop-front photographs are included. All assets are local except outbound WhatsApp, map and social links.
